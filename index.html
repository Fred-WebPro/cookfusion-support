Support

Questions? We're here.

How do I reset my password?
Open the app → Settings → Account → Change Password, and we'll email you a secure reset link. If you can't sign in, use "Forgot Password?" on the login screen — you'll receive a 6-digit code by email.
What happens to the photos I take?
Photos of your fridge or food products are processed only to identify ingredients and generate suggestions. They are never used for advertising and never shared with third parties for their own purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
How does the AI recipe generation work?
CookFusion analyzes the ingredients you list or photograph and generates recipes tailored to your dietary profile and your family members' preferences — including allergies and dislikes.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your account and all associated data are permanently removed. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.
The app isn't working correctly. What should I do?
Make sure you have the latest version and an active internet connection. If the problem persists, email us — please include your device model and what happened. We usually reply within 24 hours.
