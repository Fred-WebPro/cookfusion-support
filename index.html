Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Open the app, go to Settings → Account → Change Password, and we will send a secure reset link to your email. If you cannot sign in, use "Forgot Password?" on the login screen.

How does the AI recipe generation work?

CookFusion analyzes the ingredients you list or photograph and generates recipes tailored to your dietary profile and your family members' preferences.

What happens to the photos I take?

Photos of your fridge or food products are processed only to identify ingredients and generate suggestions. They are not shared with third parties or used for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your account and all associated data will be permanently removed. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

The app is not working correctly. What should I do?

Make sure you have the latest version installed and an active internet connection. If the problem persists, contact us below — please describe your device model and what happened.
