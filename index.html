About the app

Is CookFusion really free?

Yes. Every feature on this page works without paying: the fridge scanner, the AI chef, the planner and the shopping list. There is no subscription, no paywall and no advertising in the app.

Do I have to photograph my fridge?

No. The photo is one way in. You can also search 13,501 recipes, browse by cuisine, answer three questions and let the app pick, or ask the AI chef in plain words.

How does it handle allergies?

You add each person at your table with their own allergies, diet and dislikes. Anything that clashes is removed before you see it, not flagged afterwards. Portions scale to the number of people eating.

What happens to my fridge photos?

They are sent for ingredient recognition and nothing else. Not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties for their own purposes. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 15.1 or later. An Android version is not available yet.

Can I publish my own recipes?

Yes. Anyone can become an author, publish recipes under their own name and get a public page to share.

Support

How do I reset my password?

In the app open Settings, then Account, then Change Password, and we will email you a secure link. If you can’t sign in, tap “Forgot password?” on the login screen and you will get a 6-digit code by email.

How do I delete my account?

In the app open Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. Your account and all associated data are removed permanently. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

The app isn’t working correctly. What should I do?

Make sure you have the latest version and an internet connection. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and what happened. We usually reply within 24 hours.
